Privacy Policy

Last Updated — October 2026 Version 2.4 Jurisdiction — Global

Your privacy is treated with the same care as your interiors — with restraint, clarity and no surprises. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you hold.

InteriHub Studio (“we”, “our”) operates this website and its client portal. This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is collected, used and protected when you interact with our services.

01

Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide — when registering on the portal, submitting an inquiry, booking a consultation, or otherwise contacting us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, property location and project details. We also collect limited technical data, such as browser type and pages viewed, to keep the site secure and well-maintained.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, manage bookings and the client portal, prepare proposals, deliver project communications and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information. Period.

03

Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal information in reliance on the performance of a contract, your consent (which you may withdraw at any time), our legitimate business interests, and compliance with applicable legal obligations.

04

Sharing Your Information

Information is shared only with service providers essential to our work — such as secure hosting, payment processing and project collaboration tools — and never for advertising. Where a contractor requires access, they are bound by confidentiality obligations consistent with this policy.

05

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use a minimal set of cookies — those necessary for site function and, where you consent, for anonymous analytics. No cross-site advertising cookies are used. You may clear or block cookies through your browser at any time.

06

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as needed to fulfil the purposes described above, or as required by law. Unused portal accounts are purged after twenty-four months of inactivity.

07

International Transfers

As a studio operating across New York, London and Dubai, your data may be processed in more than one jurisdiction. Where transferred, it is protected by appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses.

08

Your Data Protection Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; object to or restrict processing; and request portability. These rights are honoured free of charge and confirmed within thirty days. To exercise any right, write to the address below.

09

Security

We employ encryption in transit, restricted access controls and routine audits to protect your information. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under sixteen, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our practice or the law evolves. Material changes will be communicated through the portal and noted at the top of this page.

12

Contact

Questions, requests or concerns regarding privacy may be addressed to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@interihub.com, or by post to InteriHub Studio, 125 Architectural Way, Suite 400, New York, NY 10012.
