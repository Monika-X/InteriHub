01 — The Brief

O

What does a room do
when the city is loud?

A Lower Manhattan penthouse reimagined as a study in restraint. Hand-plastered walls in warm grey, bookmatched Calacatta at the hearth, and a ceiling line that appears to float above the room. Light enters from two full-height glazed walls, and our only task was to keep it honest — no curtain treatment, no competing pattern, no gesture louder than the Hudson itself.

Location Manhattan, NY Duration 14 months Scope Living room & gallery Materials Calacatta, plaster, oak
